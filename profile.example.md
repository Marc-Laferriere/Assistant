Copy this file to `profile.md` and rewrite it for yourself. `profile.md` is
git-ignored. This text is sent to the AI model as context for every email
classification and every daily plan, so the more concrete it is (real project
names, real senders, real noise sources), the better the triage. Expect to
revise it a few times once real mail starts flowing.

## Important to me (surface in the brief)

- Mail from the people and systems above about an active project.
- Direct questions or requests addressed to me.
- Anything with a real deadline or a fabrication/delivery impact.

## Can wait (normal)

- Meeting minutes with nothing assigned to me.
- Vendor admin: order confirmations, renewals, license notices.

## Noise - safe to bury (low / ignore)

- Newsletters and trade publications (name the senders).
- Marketing and event promotion from vendors.
- Account/security notifications and one-time codes.
- Obvious scams.

## Signals it needs an action from me

Wording such as: please review / can you / need / when will / advise / confirm;
a drawing or information request; a delivery or deadline date; a question
aimed at me.

## Signals it's NOT a task

FYI / for your records / no reply needed; "thanks"; newsletters, receipts, OTP
codes, marketing; minutes with no action for me; automated status notices.
